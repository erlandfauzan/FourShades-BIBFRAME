Architectural Modules (The Four Shades)

Each module isolates a distinct data-engineering and quality-assurance responsibility.

Life
Naberius

Transforms flat MARCXML records into linked bf:Work, bf:Instance, and bf:Item RDF triples.

src/naberius_etl/converter.py

Istaroth

src/istaroth_lineage/lineage.py

src/asmoday_gateway/app.py
Death
Ronova
